Hi, I'm Yash. <br/>
I like building things with computers. Most of what I make sits somewhere between technology, design, and music.
I got access to a computer pretty early and was immediately fascinated by software. I still remember installing creative tools like Blender and Unity and watching the installation like magic. <br/><br/>
I started making simple [3D models](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLpJ4x1I4KOh2us8tVKkriGTfiD5r0XW93) and [animations](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLpJ4x1I4KOh16bpSaMMBqMEbO6iOOFYr6), which later pulled me into game development. I even built a small game called [Wheel Roll](https://skyash.itch.io/wheel-roll). I later moved into [web development](https://www.skyash.me/craft) and did some freelance work. Looking back, a lot of that early stuff was scrappy and lived in THE GAP.<br/> <br/>
These days I’m interested in computational media, writing, and hip-hop music. <br/>
Building at [Creative Computers](http://localhost:1313/).
